# People & Relationships · 人物与关系

> **学习原则**  
> 少记“词义”，多学“怎么说”。先掌握少量高频词，再用自然句式把它们真正说进 IELTS Speaking。

## 1 · 关系称呼 Core relationship words

只保留口语里最常用、最值得主动掌握的关系词。

| Word / phrase | 中文 | Natural use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **cousin** | 堂 / 表兄弟姐妹 | *one of my cousins* |
| **sibling** | 兄弟姐妹 | *I have two siblings.* |
| **relative** | 亲戚 | *close relatives / distant relatives* |
| **immediate family** | 直系 / 最亲近的家庭成员 | *Most of my immediate family live nearby.* |
| **extended family** | 大家庭 / 更广泛的亲属 | *I have quite a large extended family.* |
| **only child** | 独生子女 | *I’m an only child.* |

**Recognition only:** nephew / niece, father-in-law / mother-in-law, son-in-law / daughter-in-law. These are useful to recognise, but they are not priority speaking vocabulary unless they match your real life.

## 2 · 性格描述 Personality

### 优先掌握

| Word | 中文 | Useful idea |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **reliable** | 可靠的 | someone you can depend on |
| **open-minded** | 思想开放的 | willing to consider different ideas |
| **sociable** | 善于社交的 | comfortable talking to people |
| **ambitious** | 有抱负 / 上进的 | determined to achieve something |
| **blunt** | 说话过于直接的 | says exactly what they think |
| **judgemental** | 爱评判别人的 | quick to criticise other people |
| **nosy** | 爱打听别人私事的 | too interested in other people’s private lives |

### 次优先 · 认识即可

**stingy** 小气的 · **over-sensitive** 过于敏感的 · **good fun** 很有趣、很好相处的人

## 3 · 核心口语句式 Speaking patterns

### A. 直接描述一个人

**I’d describe + 人 + as + 性格词, because ...**

> *I’d describe my father as quite sociable because he finds it easy to talk to new people.*

比单独说 *My father is sociable.* 更容易自然展开。

### B. 用行为解释性格

**He / She is the kind of person who + 行为 ...**

> *She’s the kind of person who always keeps her promises.*  
> → naturally supports **reliable**.

这个句式特别适合避免“只堆形容词”。

### C. 表达你欣赏某人的地方

**What I like / admire most about + 人 + is that ...**

> *What I admire most about him is that he’s very open-minded and willing to listen to different opinions.*

### D. 描述关系亲近

**I’m quite / particularly close to + 人, and ...**

> *I’m particularly close to my mother, and I usually talk to her whenever I have something on my mind.*

### E. 描述相处方式

**I get along really well with + 人 because ...**

> *I get along really well with my cousin because we have a similar sense of humour.*

### F. 描述一个不完全正面的特点

**He / She can be a bit + 负面性格词 + sometimes, but ...**

> *My father can be a bit blunt sometimes, but I know he usually means well.*

这比直接说 *He is blunt* 更自然，也更有层次。

## 4 · 回答展开框架 How to extend an answer

人物题优先用这个简单结构：

**relationship → personality → behaviour / reason → small example**

> *She’s my cousin. I’d describe her as very sociable because she finds it easy to talk to almost anyone. Whenever we go to a family gathering, she’s usually the one who gets everyone talking.*

不需要每次四步都说满；重点是从一个词自然扩展成完整回答。

## 5 · 我的表达 ★ My expressions

`★` 标记我们在练习中根据你的真实家人、朋友和经历共同修改出的表达。它们会优先保留在对应句式下面，而不是单独堆成一大块。

例如之后你自己写：

> *My father is sociable...*

我们会把它修改成更自然的版本，再标记为：

> ★ *I’d describe my father as quite sociable because ...*

## 6 · 补充表达 Extra useful language

只在确实有用时补充，不追求数量。

- **look up to someone** — 敬佩某人  
  *I’ve always looked up to him because ...*
- **catch up with someone** — 和久未见的人聊近况  
  *I usually catch up with my cousins during the holidays.*
- **be there for someone** — 在某人需要时支持他 / 她  
  *She’s always been there for me when I needed help.*
