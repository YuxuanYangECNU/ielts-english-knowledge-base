# People & Relationships — Family Relationships

> **Quick answer**  
> Keep only family words and relationship expressions that are genuinely useful for IELTS Speaking.

## Core relationship words

| Word / phrase | 中文速览 | Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **cousin** | 堂/表兄弟姐妹 | *I’m quite close to one of my cousins.* |
| **nephew / niece** | 侄子/外甥；侄女/外甥女 | Useful for describing extended family |
| **uncle / aunt** | 叔伯舅姨等 | Common family vocabulary |
| **sibling** | 兄弟姐妹 | *I have two siblings.* |
| **relative** | 亲戚 | *close relatives / distant relatives* |

## In-law vocabulary

**in-law** refers to a family relationship created through marriage.

| Word / phrase | 中文速览 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **father-in-law / mother-in-law** | 岳父/公公；岳母/婆婆 |
| **son-in-law / daughter-in-law** | 女婿；儿媳 |
| **brother-in-law / sister-in-law** | 姻亲中的兄弟/姐妹关系 |

## Useful family-group terms

- **immediate family** — 最亲近的家庭成员
- **extended family** — 大家庭 / 更广泛的亲属
- **only child** — 独生子女
- **twin sister / twin brother** — 双胞胎姐妹 / 兄弟

## Speaking expressions

These are more useful in IELTS Speaking than memorising many kinship terms.

- **be close to someone** — 和某人关系亲近  
  *I’m particularly close to my mother.*
- **get along well with someone** — 和某人相处融洽  
  *I get along really well with my family.*
- **have a close-knit family** — 家庭关系紧密  
  *We’re quite a close-knit family.*
- **look up to someone** — 敬佩 / 尊敬某人  
  *I’ve always looked up to my father.*
