# Accommodation & Renting — Listening Vocabulary

> **Quick answer**  
> A compact vocabulary network from a housing / accommodation listening dictation. The focus is recognition, spelling and scenario grouping rather than isolated memorisation.

## 1 · Property and location

| Word / phrase | Meaning | Listening note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **insurance** | insurance; protection against financial loss | Common in rental contexts: home insurance, contents insurance |
| **outskirts** | the outer parts of a town or city; 郊区 | Usually plural: **on the outskirts of ...** |
| **cottage** | a small house, often in the countryside; 乡间小屋 / 小别墅 | Not the same as **farmhouse**, which specifically refers to a house on a farm |

## 2 · Room types

| Word / phrase | Meaning | Listening note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **bedsit** | a single room used as both a bedroom and living room; 开间 / 卧室兼起居室 | British English; may have some facilities shared |
| **en-suite / ensuite** | a bedroom with a private attached bathroom; 带独立卫浴的房间 | Do not confuse with **suite**, which is a set of connected rooms |
| **twin room** | a room with two separate single beds; 双床房 | **twin** = two separate beds |
| **triple room** | a room for three people; 三人间 | Bed arrangement may vary |

## 3 · Areas of a house

| Word / phrase | Meaning | Listening note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **sitting room** | living room; 起居室 | Common British English term |
| **foyer** | entrance hall / lobby; 门厅 / 前厅 | Often heard in hotels, theatres and larger buildings |
| **porch** | a covered entrance area outside a house; 门廊 | Not the same as **corridor / hallway** = 走廊 |

## 4 · Food and special requirements

| Word / phrase | Meaning | Listening note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **dietary requirements** | special food needs or restrictions; 特殊饮食要求 | Common in accommodation, travel and event-booking contexts |
| **food allergy** | an allergic reaction to a particular food; 食物过敏 | Often appears alongside dietary requirements |

## 5 · Furniture and household equipment

| Word / phrase | Meaning | Listening note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **radiator** | a heating unit / radiator; 暖气片 / 散热器 | Spelling: **radiator**, not *rediator* |
| **cabinet** | a storage unit with shelves, drawers or doors; 柜子 / 橱柜 | Often used for kitchen, bathroom or filing storage |
| **vacuum cleaner / vacuum** | a machine used to clean floors by suction; 吸尘器 | **vacuum** is common as a shortened everyday form |
| **fridge** | refrigerator; 冰箱 | Very common British English everyday form |
| **washbasin** | a bathroom sink; 洗脸盆 | Compound spelling: **washbasin** |
| **cupboard** | a storage space or cabinet with doors; 橱柜 / 储物柜 | Very common British English household word |
| **freezer** | an appliance or compartment for frozen food; 冰柜 / 冷冻柜 | Contrast: **fridge** keeps food cool; **freezer** keeps it frozen |

## High-value contrasts

### cabinet vs cupboard

- **cabinet** is a broad term for a storage unit and is common in expressions such as **kitchen cabinet**, **medicine cabinet** and **filing cabinet**.
- **cupboard** is especially common in British English for household storage, particularly in kitchens or built-in storage spaces.
- In some situations, both may be translated as “橱柜 / 柜子”; the exact choice depends on the object and variety of English.

### foyer vs porch vs hallway

- **foyer** → an entrance hall inside a building.
- **porch** → a covered area at the entrance, usually outside a house.
- **hallway / corridor** → a passage connecting rooms.

### twin room vs double room

- **twin room** → two separate single beds.
- **double room** → usually one double bed for two people.

## Active recall

Without looking back, explain the difference between:

1. **twin room** and **double room**;
2. **foyer**, **porch** and **hallway**;
3. **fridge** and **freezer**;
4. **cabinet** and **cupboard**.

Then try to recall all accommodation words under these headings: **room type · house area · equipment · food requirements · security**.
