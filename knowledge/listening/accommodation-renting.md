# Accommodation & Renting — Listening Vocabulary

> **Quick answer**  
> A compact vocabulary network from a housing / accommodation listening dictation. Each term now uses **Chinese quick gloss + standard English definition/context** so the meaning is fast to scan but still precise.

## 1 · Property and location

| Word / phrase | 中文速览 | English definition & context | Listening note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **insurance** | 保险 | protection against financial loss, usually provided through a policy | Common in rental contexts: **home insurance, contents insurance** |
| **outskirts** | 郊区；城市外围 | the outer parts of a town or city | Usually plural: **on the outskirts of ...** |
| **cottage** | 乡间小屋；小别墅 | a small house, often in the countryside | Different from **farmhouse**, which specifically refers to a house on a farm |

## 2 · Room types

| Word / phrase | 中文速览 | English definition & context | Listening note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **bedsit** | 开间；卧室兼起居室 | a single room used as both a bedroom and living room | British English; some facilities may be shared |
| **en-suite / ensuite** | 带独立卫浴的房间；套内卫浴 | a bedroom with a private bathroom directly attached to it | Do not confuse with **suite**, a set of connected rooms |
| **twin room** | 双床房 | a room with two separate single beds | **twin** = two separate beds |
| **triple room** | 三人间 | a room intended for three people | Bed arrangement may vary |

## 3 · Areas of a house

| Word / phrase | 中文速览 | English definition & context | Listening note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **sitting room** | 起居室 | a room used for sitting and relaxing; essentially a living room | Common British English term |
| **foyer** | 门厅；前厅；大堂 | an entrance hall or lobby inside a building | Common in hotels, theatres and larger buildings |
| **porch** | 门廊 | a covered area at the entrance to a house, usually outside | Different from **hallway / corridor** = 走廊 |

## 4 · Food and special requirements

| Word / phrase | 中文速览 | English definition & context | Listening note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **dietary requirements** | 特殊饮食要求 | food-related needs or restrictions caused by health, religion, preference or other reasons | Common in accommodation, travel and event bookings |
| **food allergy** | 食物过敏 | an allergic reaction caused by a particular food | Often appears alongside dietary requirements |

## 5 · Furniture and household equipment

| Word / phrase | 中文速览 | English definition & context | Listening note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **radiator** | 暖气片；散热器 | a device that heats a room by giving off heat | Spelling worth recognising accurately in dictation |
| **cabinet** | 柜子；橱柜 | a storage unit with shelves, drawers or doors | Common: **kitchen cabinet, medicine cabinet, filing cabinet** |
| **vacuum cleaner / vacuum** | 吸尘器 | a machine that cleans floors and surfaces by suction | **vacuum** is common as a shortened everyday form |
| **fridge** | 冰箱 | a refrigerator used to keep food and drinks cool | Very common British everyday form |
| **washbasin** | 洗脸盆 | a basin or sink used for washing your hands and face | Compound spelling: **washbasin** |
| **cupboard** | 橱柜；储物柜 | a storage space with doors, often used for food, dishes or household items | Very common British household word |
| **freezer** | 冰柜；冷冻柜 | an appliance or compartment that keeps food frozen | Contrast: **fridge** = cool; **freezer** = frozen |

## 6 · Security

| Word / phrase | 中文速览 | English definition & context | Listening note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
