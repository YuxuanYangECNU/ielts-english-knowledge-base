## High-value listening contrasts

- **hiking vs trekking** — trekking is usually longer and more demanding.
- **rock climbing vs cliff walk** — climbing a rock face vs walking along a cliff route.
- **scuba diving vs snorkeling** — underwater with breathing equipment vs near-surface breathing through a snorkel.
- **swimming trunks vs swimming costume** — men's swim shorts vs general British term for swimwear.
